# Overlay

An overlay here is a rule that generally acts for a few days around a month-end, under certain market behaviour. The general economic idea here is forced rebalancing for pension funds, index funds and other financial institutions affecting prices for reasons unrelated to their value. Similarly to sleeves, only the combined overlays are shown.

## Results

February 2001 to September 2026, from £10,000, after modelled costs:

| | CAGR | Sharpe | max drawdown | £10,000 becomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| core + defensive + rebound | 13.8% | 0.95 | −20.5% | £273,100 |
| core + defensive + rebound + overlay | 14.8% | 1.03 | −20.5% | £347,600 |

Its one notable losing year is 2016 (-2.6%), when the fallout from Brexit disrupted general month-end patterns

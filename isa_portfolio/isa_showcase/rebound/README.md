# Rebound layer

Just after a recession, markets often recover sharply as investor confidence increases, and riskier assets tend to be the biggest beneficiaries of this effect. This basket is chosen for such recovery period, where it is held for a fixed period following the defensive-exit trigger going off, before re-entering the standard core/sleeves/overlay configuration.

## Results

February 2001 to September 2026, from £10,000, after modelled costs:

| | CAGR | Sharpe | max drawdown | £10,000 becomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| core + defensive | 10.7% | 0.84 | −16.1% | £134,300 |
| core + defensive + rebound | 13.8% | 0.95 | −20.5% | £273,100 |

The deeper drawdown comes primarily from the Euro crises in 2010. At that point, the basket was still being held following the 2008/2009 recession, and the riskier assets held were the most affected.

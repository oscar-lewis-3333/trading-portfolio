Estimator definitions

Windows are 21 and 63 sessions. Require ceil(80% × window) valid bars and ceil(80% × (window−1)) adjacent pairs. CS uses the published overnight range translation and zeros negative pair spreads before averaging. AR uses the published two-day corrected version: zero negative squared pair estimates, square-root each pair, then average. EDGE uses the authors’ rolling implementation and default absolute square root. EDGE returns a root-mean-square effective-spread estimate, whereas CS/AR here average corrected pair estimates; levels are not perfectly interchangeable. No stale-bar copying or gap compression is used. Hand-checkable examples and reference-code agreement pass.

Costs and missing estimates

All estimates and peer medians are known at the prior decision close and shifted by a full exchange session. Missing estimates use the positive eligible liquidity-bucket median, then positive eligible-universe median; an explicit 30bp per-side house fallback covers a date with no positive peers. Genuine direct zero estimates remain zero under published conventions; missing estimates are never set to zero. Fallback usage is measured on executed notional. Liquidity uses the strategy’s lagged 60-session median Close × Volume in GBP. Bucket boundaries are £100k, £250k and £1m per session.
